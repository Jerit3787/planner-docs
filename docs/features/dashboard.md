# Overview and analytics

The app's **Today** and analytics views summarize your current study plan and progress. Depending on
the data you have entered, you can see today's classes, upcoming work, semester results, focus time,
and test trends.

## Study summaries

The analytics view can include a semester GPA summary, upcoming work, focus time by subject, score
trends, and revision suggestions linked to upcoming tests. Add planner records and log focus sessions
to build these summaries.

You can adjust which summary cards appear and their order from the customize control on the screen.
Your planner records and preferences save automatically.
