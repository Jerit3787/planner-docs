# Reminders

Stutastic Planner can schedule class and deadline reminders on supported native platforms. Reminders
are scheduled locally on your device, so they can fire while you are offline. They are off until you
enable them and allow notifications in your device settings.

Reminders are not available in the web app. They do not require a signed-in account.

## Turn reminders on

Open **Settings → Reminders**, turn on **Remind me**, and allow the operating system to show
notifications. If permission is denied, enable notifications for Stutastic Planner in your device's
system settings.

The app schedules reminders up to two weeks ahead. Open the app at least once every two weeks so it
can refresh that schedule.

## Choose what to be reminded about

- **Before a class starts** — choose 5, 10, 15, 30, or 60 minutes.
- **What is due today** — a reminder an hour before your first class that day.
- **What is due tomorrow** — a reminder after your last class of the day.

You can change these options or turn reminders off in **Settings → Reminders**. Turning reminders off
cancels the reminders already scheduled on that device.
