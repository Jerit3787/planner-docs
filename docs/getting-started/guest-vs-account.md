# Guest mode vs. an account

You can use Stutastic Planner without creating an account.

## Guest mode

At the end of setup, choose **Not now, keep it on this device**. Your planner records are stored on
that device and are not synced to an account. Guest mode works offline, but uninstalling the app,
clearing its local data, or losing the device can remove the only copy of those records. Use
[Export everything](../data/export-import.md) to keep a backup.

## Using an account

Sign in with Google or email; Apple sign-in is available on Apple platforms. An account lets the app
sync planner records across devices when they are online. A network connection is needed to sign in
and sync.

If you start as a guest, you can create an account later from **Settings → Account** using
**Create an account to sync**. Your existing planner data comes with you. You can also export a backup
and restore it on another device; see [Export and Import](../data/export-import.md).

## Signing out or deleting data

**Leave without saving** ends a guest session. It asks first, then deletes everything the guest
stored on the device. Create an account first if you want to keep it.

For a signed-in account, **Sign out** keeps the cloud account, while **Delete my account** removes
the account and its synced planner records.

Leaving guest mode, signing out and deleting an account all leave the device as if the app had just
been installed:
- planner data is removed from the device;
- university logins kept for refreshing your timetable are deleted;
- scheduled reminders are cancelled;
- your settings on that device go back to their defaults: theme, clock, reminders and layout.

Only your choice about usage analytics is kept. Export a backup first if you
want to keep a copy. Account deletion also attempts to disconnect Google Calendar. If the app cannot
confirm cleanup, deletion stops so you can retry or disconnect from **Settings → Google Calendar**.
