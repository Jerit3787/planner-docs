# Support

Use the [Stutastic Planner support tracker](https://support.danplace.tech/p/stu) to search known
issues, vote for one that affects you, or report a new bug or feature request. You don't need an
account, and the team replies through the tracker.

## Before you report

- Check the [FAQ](faq.md) and search the support tracker first — the issue may already be known and
  have a workaround.
- Update to the latest version of the app and try again.
- Note your app version from **Settings → About**, your device model, your operating system or
  browser version, and whether you use the web/PWA, iPhone/iPad, Android, or desktop app.
- Write down the steps that led to the problem, what you expected to happen, and what actually
  happened.
- Screenshots, recordings, or relevant log files help. Remove personal or study information you don't
  need to share.

## Report a bug or request a feature

1. Open [support.danplace.tech/p/stu](https://support.danplace.tech/p/stu).
2. Search for an existing issue and vote for it, or choose **Report a bug**.
3. Fill in the area, summary, what happened, optional steps to reproduce, expected and actual
   results, how much it affects you, and your environment if it is relevant.
4. Choose **Public** or **Private**. Public reports, their replies, and their attachments are visible
   to everyone. Use **Private** for account details, personal information, or anything you don't want
   public.
5. Enter your name and email address. Your email is never shown publicly; it is used to send you a
   private link to follow the report and to email you when its status changes.
6. Submit the report. Open the private link in the email to follow replies or add more detail. If you
   lose the email, use [Email me my links](https://support.danplace.tech/links) to get a fresh private
   link for every report submitted from your address.

You can attach up to five files, up to 15 MB each: images, video, PDF, text, or log files. Don't
upload passwords, sign-in codes, or private academic records.

## Follow-up and privacy

- The tracker is a separate service from your Stutastic Planner account. See the
  [Privacy Policy](data/privacy-policy.md#support-tracker) for what it stores and how to ask for
  deletion.
- If you can't sign in to delete your account, submit a **Private** report from the address on the
  account and ask us to delete it. Don't include your password.
- For a privacy request about the app or the tracker, use a **Private** report as well.

For account, sync, calendar, or notification questions, the [FAQ](faq.md) and the rest of this site
may answer it without waiting for a reply.
