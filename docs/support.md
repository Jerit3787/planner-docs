# Support

Need help with Stutastic Planner? Email [support@danplace.tech](mailto:support@danplace.tech).

To help us understand an issue, include:

- Your Stutastic Planner app version.
- Your device model and operating system version.
- What you were trying to do and what happened.
- The steps needed to reproduce the issue, if you know them.

Please don't email passwords, sign-in codes, or private academic records. If you attach a screenshot,
remove any personal or study information that isn't needed to explain the issue.

For common questions, see the [FAQ](faq.md).
